# Playtest: the anchor moment (story 071, AC-66 [PT], P-16)

**Status: NOT RUN.** This is the kit and the blank record. It needs five real first-time players, so it cannot be automated. It gates the first-playable milestone.

Story: `production/epics/sprite-camera-presentation/story-071-the-anchor-moment-playtest.md`

## Before the session

- **Players:** five people who have never seen the game, not the developer, and not anyone who has watched it being built. Run them one at a time, out of earshot of each other.
- **Build:** double-click `play.bat` at the repo root. It boots Title, then New Game, then the overworld. Walking into an enemy symbol starts the first battle, and the Supervisor is the first boss.
- **Setup:** windowed 1280x720 or larger, with sound on. Keyboard is fine; offer a gamepad if the player prefers one.
- **Gear:** a phone timer for the 5-second check, and this sheet.

## What you say (only this)

> "This is a game in development. Play it however you like. I can't answer questions while you play. Please think out loud if you can."

Give no explanation of the controls, the factions, the rules or what's coming. If the player is stuck for more than 60 s, you may say "try the arrow keys and Enter" once. Record it.

## The three checks (score each independently)

| # | Moment | When | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The first frame reads** | Start the timer as the first battle wipe opens. Within **5 s**, ask: "What's going on? Who's who?" | **4 of 5** point to "my side" and "the enemies" and say they're in a fight |
| 2 | **The first reaction lands** | The first reaction frame of the player's first hit (the enemy's hurt pose) | **3 of 5** visibly react: a smile, a laugh or a comment |
| 3 | **The boss reads** | After the boss takeover finishes. If they say nothing unprompted, ask: "What changed?" | **4 of 5** say the fight escalated, or that the enemy's side took over the place |

**Write down each player's exact words**, not only pass or fail. A revision needs to know which cue failed to read.

## If a threshold is missed

It is logged as a **design problem** against the GDD's Player Fantasy, staging or lighting, not as a bug. The first-playable gate stays blocked until the design is revised and a rerun with **five new players** passes.
